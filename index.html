02 / Start with the details

How to use an OopBuy spreadsheet

An OopBuy spreadsheet is a product research list that helps you organize finds and revisit their source listings. This page provides an independent CAFANS-based starting point. A product appearing here does not establish its availability through a particular agent.

01 — DISCOVER

Choose a useful category

Filter the directory by product type, then compare similar options. Record the exact color, size and quantity you want rather than relying on a product name alone.

02 — VERIFY

Open the source listing

Check the current product page for the selected option, seller information, measurements and price. Keep unanswered questions in your shortlist until you can confirm them.

03 — PLAN

Confirm the buying route

If using an agent, confirm it accepts the original listing before paying. Review its current service terms, inspection options, fees and shipping quote for your destination.

03 / Before you decide

Turn a find into a useful comparison

Fit and product details

  • Footwear: record the sizing system, width notes and any supplied insole measurements.
  • T-shirts and sweatshirts: compare chest width and body length with a garment you already wear.
  • Materials: save the seller’s stated composition; appearance alone cannot confirm fabric or leather type.
  • Options: keep the chosen size, color and number of pieces beside the listing link.

Photos and inspection notes

  • Distinguish listing photographs from photos of the actual item being purchased.
  • When inspection photos are available, examine seams, closures, visible marks and the selected option.
  • Ask for a measurement photo if a missing dimension could change your decision.
  • Photos can reveal visible issues but do not establish authenticity, durability or complete product quality.

05 / Quick answers

OopBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official OopBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. Its main shopping buttons lead to CAFANS, and the directory contains CAFANS source listings. It does not process orders or provide OopBuy customer support.

Are these live prices and in-stock products?

No. The table is a manually imported snapshot of six CAFANS listings with reference USD estimates. Availability, product options and final amounts must be checked on the current source page.

Can I order every listing through OopBuy?

That has not been verified. Open the CAFANS product details, locate the original seller link where available, and confirm that your chosen agent accepts it before adding funds or placing an order.

Is there a downloadable spreadsheet?

With JavaScript enabled, the export button downloads the currently visible rows as a CSV file. It includes product names, categories, reference prices and the available CAFANS source links. The CSV is a research snapshot, not a live inventory feed.

How should I compare sizing?

Use the measurement guidance for the exact listing and selected option. Compare equivalent measurements in the same units with a well-fitting item you own. A size label by itself is not enough to establish fit.

Does the listed item price include shipping?

The amounts shown here are product reference estimates. Do not assume they include domestic delivery, international freight, service fees or taxes. Use the worksheet to record your own estimates and confirm the final quote with the relevant provider.
